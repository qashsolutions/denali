How to read these:
  • Your health and Mood detail are buildable now on your existing screener data — the only new piece is the sparkline (increment 2) and the detail-screen restructure.
  • Blood pressure is the Tier-2 ceiling: it shows what a continuous marker looks like once there's a data feed (HealthKit / Health Connect or manual entry) plus sourced reference tables. I used blood pressure rather than cardio fitness since you noted that was just an example — the layout is identical for weight, labs, or resting heart rate.
  • Deliberately restrained: screeners get a calm dot-trend, never a steps-style bar chart — sparse ordinal data can't honestly support that, and a dramatic mental-health trend would be the wrong call clinically. Boldness is spent only on the ridgeline signature and the single teal accent.
  • Every clinical line still carries the ‡ provisional mark, and the disclaimer is pinned once per screen.
